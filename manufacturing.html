Technical Execution

End-to-End Composite Production Workflow

Every stage of production is monitored to guarantee mechanical performance, fire rating, and visual fidelity.

01 ±0.5mm CNC Routing

Mould Design & Tooling

Master pattern fabrication utilizing CNC routing on high-density polyurethane tooling board or precision joinery. Composite production moulds are engineered to deliver dimensional tolerances within ±0.5mm across complex architectural curvatures.

02 0.5–0.8mm UV Barrier

UV Gelcoat Application

Application of calibrated 0.5mm to 0.8mm isophthalic or vinyl ester gelcoat barriers enriched with UV-absorbers. Formulated to provide decades of resistance against chalking, micro-cracking, and color fading under Saudi desert sunlight.

03 Resin-to-Glass Optimization

Lamination & Compaction

Manual and spray-assisted lay-up of chopped strand mat (CSM) and multi-axial continuous woven roving glass fibers. Interlaminar air voids are eliminated using grooved consolidation rollers to achieve optimal resin-to-glass ratios.

04 Rigid PU & Steel Inserts

Structural Core & Curing

Insertion of rigid high-density polyurethane (PU) foam cores or encapsulated structural metal brackets where additional flexural stiffness or sound dampening is required. Curing takes place under controlled exotherm conditions.

05 SS316 Fixing Integration

Post-Trimming & Assembly

Precision perimeter trimming, CNC fastener hole drilling, gasket channel milling, and bonding of concealed stainless steel (SS316) fixing brackets for rapid on-site contractor installation.

06 ASTM D2583 Barcol Verified

QA & Hardness Audits

Barcol hardness testing (ASTM D2583) to verify cross-linking cure maturity, ultrasonic laminate thickness profiling, and visual surface inspection before crating and dispatch to project sites.

Material Science

Formulation & Material Options

Selected based on project-specific environmental, chemical, and fire resistance criteria.

Resin Systems

Polymer Matrices for Extreme Climates
  • Isophthalic Polyester Architectural Standard

    Standard high-performance resin offering superior water resistance, weatherability, and mechanical strength for architectural cladding and decorative pillar wraps.

  • Vinyl Ester Acid & Chemical Grade

    Superior chemical and acid resistance for harsh petrochemical facilities, industrial wastewater basins, drainage channels, and scrubbers.

  • Fire-Retardant Formulations BS 476 / ASTM E84

    Formulated with alumina trihydrate (ATH) and halogenated flame retardants to achieve BS 476 Part 7 Class 1 and ASTM E84 Class A ratings with reduced smoke toxicity.

  • Food-Grade / Potable Systems WRAS & SASO Certified

    Non-toxic, taint-free resin systems compliant with WRAS and SASO drinking water containment standards for commercial sectional water tanks.

Reinforcements & Core Materials

Structural Glass & Thermal Cores
  • E-Glass Chopped Strand Mat (CSM) Multidirectional Strength

    Provides uniform multidirectional tensile strength, crack bridging, and impermeable laminate bulk across complex geometric panel surfaces.

  • Continuous Woven Roving & Multi-Axial Seismic & Wind Resistance

    High-tensile continuous filament glass fabrics oriented in 0°/90° or ±45° alignments engineered to absorb extreme wind suction and seismic structural loads.

  • Polyurethane (PU) Foam Core Thermal U < 0.6 W/m²·K

    Closed-cell rigid polyurethane foam sandwich core providing high flexural stiffness with minimal weight addition, achieving exceptional thermal and acoustic insulation.

  • Structural Metal Inserts SS316 / Hot-Dip Galvanized

    Encapsulated stainless steel (SS316) and aluminum load-bearing plates laminated directly into composite return flanges for secure contractor mechanical fixing.
