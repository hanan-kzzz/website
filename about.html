Company Overview

A Focused B2B Composite Fabricator in Riyadh

Gulf Fiber and Metal Company Limited operates from its corporate facility located on Al Farazdaq Street in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. We specialize in the design, tooling, moulding, and custom fabrication of high-performance Glass Reinforced Plastic (GRP) products tailored to the demanding engineering standards of the Kingdom's construction sector.

Composite materials play a transformative role in contemporary architecture and municipal civil engineering. By delivering material systems that are up to 75% lighter than precast concrete or natural stone, completely impervious to coastal salinity and chemical oxidation, and capable of enduring continuous 55°C desert surface temperatures, Gulf Fiber and Metal delivers long-term durability with minimal lifecycle maintenance.

Precision CNC Mould Tooling & Plug Making Custom parametric curvature, 3D textured facades, fluted column wraps, and Islamic heritage mashrabiya panels.
Engineered for Saudi Desert & Marine Climates Formulated with UV absorbers and non-hygroscopic resins to prevent surface chalking, blistering, or delamination.
Comprehensive Technical Submittals & Shop Drawings Complete structural engineering calculations, dead-load assessments, wind suction evaluations, and ASTM test certificates.

Our Manufacturing Philosophy

We approach composite manufacturing as a rigorous discipline combining structural fiber orientation, resin formulation chemistry, and precision mould tooling. Every component is engineered to provide predictable tensile, flexural, and fire-resistant behavior.

Quality Assurance & Testing Protocol

From raw material resin viscosity audits to ultrasound laminate thickness measurement, Barcol hardness indentation testing, and post-cure thermal conditioning, our inspection workflow ensures compliance with project specifications prior to site dispatch.

Engineering Superiority

The GRP Composite Advantage

Why tier-1 contractors, architects, and consultants specify Glass Reinforced Plastic over traditional building materials.

Structural Load

75% Weight Reduction

Slashes dead loads on superstructures and foundations. Eliminates heavy steel substructures and permits installation using smaller mobile cranes, drastically accelerating project timelines.

Dead Load: 15–20 kg/m² vs 120 kg/m² Precast
Corrosion Proof

100% Saline & Chemical Immunity

Completely unaffected by marine coastal salinity, atmospheric sulfur, or chemical washdowns. Zero spalling, rebar oxidation, or salt crystallization issues that plague conventional precast concrete.

Durability: ASTM B117 & ISO 12944 Certified
Parametric 3D

Infinite Architectural Freedom

Moulded seamlessly into fluid compound curves, geometric origami panels, fluted column wraps, and traditional Najdi mashrabiya lattice patterns with zero visible joints.

Geometry: Double-Curved & 3D Parametric
Tested Fire Safety
